# Retention Strategy Report

## Project

Customer Churn Analytics Platform – Part 2: RFM Segmentation & Retention Strategy

## Objective

The purpose of this report is to identify which customer groups require retention attention and recommend practical actions that can reduce churn while maximizing business value.

Customer segments were created using:

* Recency (How recently customers purchased)
* Frequency (How often customers purchase)
* Monetary Value (How much customers spend)

Additional signals used:

* Support ticket activity
* Customer engagement behavior
* Campaign response patterns
* Purchase habits

---

# Executive Summary

The segmentation analysis identified several customer groups with very different retention needs.

The highest-priority groups are:

1. High-Support Needs Customers
2. Discount-Sensitive Customers
3. At-Risk Customers

These groups exhibit the highest churn rates and therefore provide the greatest opportunity for retention improvement.

Meanwhile, Loyal Customers and Potential High-Value Customers should be nurtured to increase long-term revenue and customer lifetime value.

---

# Segment Profiles & Recommended Actions

## 1. Loyal Customers

### Segment Overview

* Customers: 968
* Churn Rate: 1%
* Average Customer Value: ₹644
* Median Frequency: 6 purchases

### What This Means

These customers purchase regularly, spend more than average, and rarely churn.

They represent the most stable and valuable customer group.

### Recommended Actions

* Loyalty rewards
* Early access offers
* VIP customer programs
* Referral incentives

### Business Goal

Increase customer lifetime value and encourage advocacy.

### Priority

🟡 Medium

---

## 2. Potential High-Value Customers

### Segment Overview

* Customers: 545
* Churn Rate: 18%
* Average Customer Value: ₹382
* Median Frequency: 4 purchases

### What This Means

These customers are showing strong purchasing behavior but have not yet reached Loyal Customer status.

### Recommended Actions

* Personalized product recommendations
* Cross-sell opportunities
* Educational campaigns
* Membership upgrades

### Business Goal

Convert these customers into Loyal Customers.

### Priority

🟡 Medium

---

## 3. At-Risk Customers

### Segment Overview

* Customers: 986
* Churn Rate: 44%
* Average Customer Value: ₹239
* Median Recency: 465 days

### What This Means

These customers have significantly reduced activity and may leave soon if no action is taken.

### Recommended Actions

* Personalized re-engagement emails
* Time-limited discounts
* Customer success outreach
* Reminder campaigns

### Business Goal

Re-activate customers before churn occurs.

### Priority

🔴 High

---

## 4. Dormant Customers

### Segment Overview

* Customers: 1,618
* Churn Rate: 31%
* Average Customer Value: ₹339
* Median Recency: 933 days

### What This Means

These customers have been inactive for a very long time.

Many have already mentally disconnected from the business.

### Recommended Actions

* Win-back campaigns
* Strong promotional offers
* Reactivation surveys
* New product announcements

### Business Goal

Recover customers who still have potential value.

### Priority

🔴 High

---

## 5. Discount-Sensitive Customers

### Segment Overview

* Customers: 291
* Churn Rate: 52%
* Average Customer Value: ₹195

### What This Means

These customers tend to purchase only when discounts are available.

Without incentives, they are highly likely to leave.

### Recommended Actions

* Personalized coupons
* Loyalty points programs
* Bundled offers
* Subscription discounts

### Business Goal

Reduce dependency on promotions and improve long-term loyalty.

### Priority

🔴 High

---

## 6. High-Support Needs Customers

### Segment Overview

* Customers: 592
* Churn Rate: 88%
* Average Support Tickets: 3.42

### What This Means

This is the most concerning segment.

Customers in this group experience repeated problems and are extremely likely to churn.

### Recommended Actions

* Priority support queue
* Dedicated customer success outreach
* Resolution follow-up process
* Root-cause investigation

### Business Goal

Reduce customer frustration and improve satisfaction.

### Priority

🚨 Critical

---

# Campaign Budget Allocation

Assume a limited retention budget of ₹100,000.

The budget should focus on customers where retention can generate the greatest business return.

| Segment                        | Budget Allocation |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| High-Support Needs             | 35%               |
| At-Risk Customers              | 30%               |
| Dormant Customers              | 20%               |
| Discount-Sensitive Customers   | 10%               |
| Potential High-Value Customers | 5%                |

### Why?

These groups represent the highest churn risk and therefore provide the largest opportunity for revenue protection.

---

# Expected Business Impact

Successful implementation of these recommendations can:

* Reduce customer churn
* Improve customer satisfaction
* Increase customer lifetime value
* Improve campaign efficiency
* Protect recurring revenue

---

# Conclusion

Customer churn is concentrated within a small number of high-risk segments.

The most urgent attention should be directed toward High-Support Needs, At-Risk, Dormant, and Discount-Sensitive customers.

At the same time, Loyal and Potential High-Value customers should be nurtured to maximize future revenue and long-term business growth.
